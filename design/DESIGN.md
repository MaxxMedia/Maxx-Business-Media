## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the high-stakes precision, architectural gravitas, and kinetic momentum of global enterprise trade shows, industrial conferences, and high-level B2B trade platforms. The identity positions the enterprise as an authoritative market maker connecting titans of manufacturing, automation, supply chain, and global commerce.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Industrial-Tech Precision** with **Corporate Modernist Discipline**:
- **Authoritative & Architectural:** Layouts rely on structured vertical divisions, bold tabular frameworks, and confident editorial anchors inspired by physical trade pavilion directory systems and executive dossiers.
- **Engineered Restraint:** Visual power is derived from high-contrast typography, strict geometric alignment, and deliberate negative space, avoiding ornamental fluff.
- **Dynamic Industrial Energy:** High-visibility safety and energy accents (vibrant engineered orange) pierce through deep architectural charcoals and clean chalk-toned canvas surfaces to guide decision-makers directly to high-value conversion points (e.g., booth bookings, delegate passes, sponsorship RFPs).

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable industrial signature against a tactile architectural canvas. 

### Roles & Semantic Implementation
- **Primary Orange (`#F47721`):** The primary kinetic signal. Reserved for critical transactional actions (booth reservation, ticket tier booking, primary interactive highlights, and focal data metrics). Never diluted across passive background areas.
- **Tertiary Dark Orange (`#D95D0F`):** Hover, active, and depressed states for primary interactive elements, as well as high-emphasis badge accents.
- **Secondary Charcoal (`#17191C`):** Grounded executive core. Dictates primary headings, main structural navigation shells, footer anchors, and dominant contrasting text.
- **Secondary Graphite Neutral (`#30343A`):** Used for mid-tier visual framing, icons, and emphasized UI structural outlines.
- **Secondary Text (`#666B73`):** Neutral slate tone dedicated to body copy, metadata, speaker bios, and technical parameter labels, calibrated for WCAG AAA contrast against light grounds.
- **Background Chalk (`#F7F7F5`):** A slightly warmed, desaturated concrete/paper ground that softens digital glare under long B2B procurement sessions.
- **Surface Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated cards, floor-plan viewport panels, modals, and input fields.
- **Subtle Border (`#E6E7E8`):** Architectural boundary lines, dividing modular trade grid cells and data matrices without introducing visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the bold, geometric technicality of **Sora** with the hyper-rationalised reading cadence of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings (Sora):** Utilized for structural anchors, exhibition titles, category codes, and impactful key metrics. Sora's geometric apertures project engineering rigor and technological scale. Display and primary headlines feature aggressive negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic executive physical publication spreads.
- **Body & Numerical Data (Inter):** Engineered for dense data consumption, schedule timetables, exhibitor specifications, and B2B contract terms. Line heights are tuned generously to promote fatigue-free legibility in content-dense layouts.
- **System Labels & Badges:** `label-caps` enforces uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing (`+0.08em`), evoking industrial machinery model plates, trade hall wayfinding signage, and event schedule time stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a rigorous 8px spatial grid, engineered to handle multifaceted B2B content—from interactive 3D floor plans and symposium agendas to multi-parameter exhibitor rosters.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with a 1360px maximum content envelope. Gutters are fixed to `2rem` (`32px`), with page lateral margins of `3rem` (`48px`). Sections use vertical rhythmic intervals of 80px or 120px to establish clear thematic divisions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Complex comparison tables convert into horizontally swipeable cards with anchored reference columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) canvas margins. Navigation collapses to a high-contrast executive bottom sheet or persistent anchored CTA bar for badge registrations.

### Spacing Application
- Component internal padding strictly uses multiples of 8px (`space-xs` = 8px, `space-sm` = 12px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px).
- Tight information pairings (e.g., date-location pairings, booth coordinates) employ `space-xs` or `space-sm`.
- Card modules and distinct content sections use `space-lg` and `space-xl` to maintain clean separation without relying on heavy visual borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects excessive, blurry skeuomorphism in favor of **Architectural Precision Elevation**: an orchestrated combination of surface tinting, hairline boundaries, and sharp, restrained ambient shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Background Chalk (`#F7F7F5`). The non-interactive ground for all structural layout elements.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Modular Cards / Panels):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E6E7E8`. Shadows are kept exceptionally subtle: `0 1px 3px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Hover States & Active Cards):** Elevated Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) utilizing a razor-sharp, offset shadow: `0 4px 8px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.06), 0 12px 24px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.06)`. Border shifts dynamically to Secondary Graphite (`#30343A`) or Primary Orange (`#F47721`) depending on element importance.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays, Modals, Floor Plan Tooltips):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px `#30343A` (at 15% opacity) and a commanding drop shadow: `0 16px 36px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(23, 25, 28, 0.08)`.
- **Industrial Contrast Accents:** Critical sticky navigation bars or conversion footers may utilize Secondary Charcoal (`#17191C`) with pure white text and Primary Orange CTA buttons to project structural authority.

## Shapes

The design system employs a curated curvature profile balancing engineered durability with contemporary digital refinement:

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to standard interactive controls, form inputs, secondary badges, segmented controls, and button components. Reflects industrial beveling and structural precision.
- **Large Radius (12px / `0.75rem`):** Applied to content cards, conference agenda containers, media showcase viewports, and exhibitor highlight tiles. Delivers tactile cohesion without compromising high data density.
- **Full Radius (Pill / 9999px):** Strictly reserved for dynamic status indicators (e.g., "LIVE NOW", "BOOTH CONFIRMED", "SOLD OUT") and high-priority floating category filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Primary Orange (`#F47721`), typography in `label-md` (`#FFFFFF`), 8px border radius. Minimum height: 48px for desktop (44px for touch). Hover shifts to Dark Orange (`#D95D0F`) with a micro-translation of -1px on the Y-axis. Active states apply a 1px inset shadow.
- **Secondary (Executive) Button:** Solid Charcoal (`#17191C`), text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#30343A`. Used for corporate prospectus downloads and formal inquiries.
- **Tertiary / Outline Button:** Transparent ground, 1.5px border in `#30343A`, text `#17191C`. On hover, fills with `#17191C` with text shifting to `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Exhibition Showcases
- White ground (`#FFFFFF`), 12px corner radius, 1px border `#E6E7E8`. Internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`).
- Interactive exhibition cards feature an architectural metadata header (e.g., Hall Number, Booth ID styled in `label-caps` in `#666B73`), followed by a `headline-sm` title in `#17191C`. 
- On hover, the top border activates a 3px Primary Orange accent line, shifting elevation to Tier 2.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height: 48px. White background with a 1px solid `#E6E7E8` border, 8px radius, text in `body-md` (`#17191C`).
- Focused states feature a 1.5px border in Primary Orange (`#F47721`) paired with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(244, 119, 33, 0.15)`).
- Field labels are set in `label-sm` using Secondary Charcoal (`#17191C`), paired with helper text in Secondary Text (`#666B73`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chips:** 8px radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E6E7E8`, text `#30343A` (`label-sm`). When selected, background transitions to Secondary Charcoal (`#17191C`) with `#FFFFFF` text and zero border.
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped, 4px vertical by 10px horizontal padding. "Confirmed/Open": `#EBF7EE` ground with `#1C7332` text. "Filling Fast": `#FEF2E8` ground with `#D95D0F` text.

### Data Lists & Timetables
- Agenda and conference session lists rely on a structured 3-column layout: Timestamp (`Sora` font, bold Charcoal), Session Meta & Speaker Detail (`Inter` regular), and Status/Bookmark Action.
- Separated by clean, full-width hairline rules (`#E6E7E8`). Alternating rows utilize an ultra-faint surface tint (`#FAF9F7`) for high-speed scanning during live expos.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20x20px dimension with a 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. 
- 1.5px border in Secondary Graphite (`#30343A`). Checked state fills with Primary Orange (`#F47721`) containing a crisp white tick/dot.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Interactive Floor Plan Mini-HUD:** Floating, compact Tier 3 panel containing hall selector, scale controls, and instant booth reservation summaries.
- **Exhibitor Tier Badge:** Monospace/geometric index badge (`HALL 04 — STAND C12`) designed to replicate physical industrial badge lanyards and trade wayfinding plates.
